Today we took a day trip to [Dachau Concentration Camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dachau_concentration_camp). This was the 1st and model camp for all of the other concentration camps. The camp had prisoners from 1933 up till 1945. It is hard to explain how powerful it is to walk on the grounds of Dachau. Pictures don’t do it justice, but I believe I did a good job being respectful while capturing the horrors of Dachau.
